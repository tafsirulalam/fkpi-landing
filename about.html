ABOUT FACTORKPI

We believe better visibility leads to better performance

Explore FactorKPI Book a Demo

FactorKPI was created to help organizations move beyond disconnected spreadsheets, delayed reporting, and unclear performance ownership. We bring KPIs, daily operations, workforce activity, and reporting into one connected system.

OUR STORY

Built around a simple operational problem

Organizations often know what they want to achieve, but struggle to connect those goals with the work happening every day. Performance information becomes fragmented across teams, reporting becomes manual, and accountability becomes difficult to maintain.

FactorKPI was designed around the idea that KPI management should be part of daily operations—not a separate reporting exercise. By connecting operational activity with measurable outcomes, organizations can understand performance while work is happening, not weeks later.

Our focus is to make structured performance management practical enough for everyday teams while providing the visibility managers and leadership need to make better decisions.

The Challenge

Fragmented operational data and manual KPI reporting.

The Idea

Connect everyday work directly with measurable performance.

The Platform

Bring KPIs, workflows, employees, production, and reporting together.

The Direction

Help organizations build continuously improving performance cultures.
